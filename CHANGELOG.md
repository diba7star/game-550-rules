# Changelog

All notable changes to the official rules of the card game **550** are recorded here.
The rules follow simple version numbers: `MAJOR.MINOR`.

## v1.0 — 2026-09-11

First publication of the complete rules.

- First complete written version of the rules of 550, in Persian and English.
- 550 is built on a simple traditional card game played informally in Iran. Its bidding and scoring system — half bids with whole-number partnership totals, the partner-confirmed blind bid, the forced minimum contract of 2 with its redeal, the trump-lead restrictions, the joker variant and the table options — was developed by Danial Diba, who also wrote the rules down and published them.
- Published on 11 September 2026 (۲۰ شهریور ۱۴۰۵).
- Text licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- Archived with a permanent DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22713387
- Online version launched the same day at https://550.hi1.ir
